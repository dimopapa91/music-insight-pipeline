What is a taste profile?

A taste profile is an AI-written summary of the music you gravitate towards: the genres, sounds and moods that connect the artists you explore, plus ideas for what to discover next.

1

Create an account and search a few artists you love.

2

Waveline links your searches to your profile (only the artists and their top tracks, nothing sensitive).

3

We generate a short, plain-language read on your taste, refreshed as you explore more.

An account keeps your profile personal to you, so it reflects your listening rather than everyone's.
