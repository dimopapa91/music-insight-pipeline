Waveline × nsgoods

Waveline's Agent API sells artist insights to AI agents over x402, for 0.005 USDC per insight on Base. Dimos Papageorgiou built the endpoint; it was built in collaboration with nsgoods, who checked it from the outside on testnet and mainnet before it opened, made its first mainnet payment, and contributed the docs. nsgoods Watchdog checks its payment terms every 15 minutes.

nsgoods runs twelve signed x402 pay-per-call APIs for AI agents on Base and Solana, and checks and monitors other x402 endpoints from the outside.
